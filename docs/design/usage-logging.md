# Hosted activity and research reports

The collaborative service records categorical operations and confirmed research
contributions through PostgreSQL. Foreground activity intervals are calibrated
and deduplicated across tabs; inactive time is not extrapolated. Administrative
reports distinguish saved contributions, checkpoints and visits. Historical
active hours cannot be reconstructed from older events.

Use the authenticated team panel's all-time report export. Browser telemetry does
not grant editorial approval and never substitutes for source/evidence records.
See [server contract](collab-server.md) and [authentication](../authentication.md).
The former desktop JSONL recorder, local UsagePanel and CLI report are retired.
Existing historical profiles and already imported provenance remain untouched.
